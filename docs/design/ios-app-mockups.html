The keystone idiom.

Walls of warm sandstone. Inked details. One keystone — a single purposeful accent that ties every screen together. Modest, precise, built to last the decade your hub will live through.

Color

7 · what to discuss

Open questions.

This is a preview, not a freeze. Things to argue about before we commit to a direction.

  1. Group by room. ✓ confirmed — Home is rooms-first, each room is a section with all its devices. v0.2 has Living Room / Kitchen / Bedroom; we will add Hallway + Outdoor as the real device set grows.
  2. Icons: SF Symbols-style stroke glyphs. ✓ confirmed — every device uses a stroke glyph (lamp, plug, sensor, curtain…). We replace the few unicode markers (●⊕▶) with consistent SVG paths. Closed/curtain glyph was added as a new one for the Bedroom mockup.
  3. Schedule form: sheet vs. push. ✓ both variants mocked (4.b section, two phones side by side). A · sheet: 3-step wizard, step indicator, Cancel one tap away. B · push: full scrollable page, live English preview at the bottom. You pick which ships.
  4. iPad. repeat the phone layout for v1 — no sidebar yet. (Easy to add later; the hub itself doesn't need iPad to be useful.)
  5. Setup screen. separate iteration — the handoff from hub-AP to home Wi-Fi is a wizard of its own and gets its own mockup pass before lock-in.
  6. Is "Ember" the right accent? Terracotta sets keystone apart from Apple Home's blue but it eats a lot of attention. Alternative: a quieter slate accent for affordance, letting the room photos do the talking. Worth a warm vs. cool A/B.
  7. Color light picker. Circular HSL is iconic but only useful with an HSL bulb; we may also have XY bulbs. Do we ship one picker or two?
  8. Always-on wall backgrounds? Apple Weather / Home go for photo backgrounds that change. We don't have room photos. Mock gradients (above) are a placeholder; do we ship it without photos, or wait?
  9. Dark by default? Living-room dim lighting favors dark; the spec leaves it to system. Do we show both, or lead with dark like Apple Home?
